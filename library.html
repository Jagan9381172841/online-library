About Our Library

Swamy Vivekananda Library is a peaceful place for students, readers and knowledge seekers. Our library provides a wide collection of educational, inspirational, historical and fiction books.

Our goal is to encourage reading habits and create an environment where everyone can learn, explore and grow.

📋 Library Instructions

  1. Maintain silence inside the library.
  2. Carry your library membership card.
  3. Do not damage or tear library books.
  4. Return books before the due date.
  5. Keep mobile phones on silent mode.
  6. Do not eat or drink near books.
  7. Keep books in their proper place.
  8. Maintain cleanliness inside the library.
  9. Follow the instructions given by library staff.
  10. Respect other readers and maintain discipline.
